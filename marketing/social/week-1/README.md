# Linguini week-one social pack

This folder contains the ready-to-post first-week campaign for X, TikTok, LinkedIn and Instagram. The week is designed as one campaign rather than four unrelated feeds: introduce the photo-led idea, show the loop, then ask people what they would photograph.

## Upload notes

- Add a currently trending sound natively in TikTok or Instagram at low volume; the cuts do not depend on a specific copyrighted sound.
- Keep auto-captions off unless you add spoken audio. All required meaning is already on screen.
- Instagram Story images are supplied as backgrounds. Place them on a 9:16 canvas in Instagram and add the named native poll/question/link sticker.
- Replace `[TRACKED LINK]` with the live campaign URL before posting.
