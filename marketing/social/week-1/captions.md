## LinkedIn

### Tuesday — why start with a photo?

Most language-learning products begin with a curriculum. Linguini begins with context.

A learner photographs a place from their day. The app suggests useful objects, the learner reviews what matters, and those approved words become a short lesson with vocabulary practice and I-Spy.

The photo is not decoration. It gives the words a memory: the desk where you worked, the café you visited, or the street you walked down.

That is the product premise we are testing in public beta: can familiar places make new vocabulary easier to notice and return to?

Try it: [TRACKED LINK]

#LanguageLearning #EdTech #ProductDesign #BuildInPublic

### Thursday — the role of AI

For a photo-led language lesson, object detection is only a suggestion layer.

Before a Linguini lesson begins, the learner can review the proposed objects, remove mistakes, and add something useful that was missed. Only the confirmed words should shape the lesson.

That interaction reflects a broader product principle for us: AI can accelerate the first draft, but the learner should keep control of what becomes learning material.

What would you always want to review before an AI-generated lesson starts?
