## 🎬 Video Asset Details
* **Composition:** `marketing/videos/linkedin-trailer/index.html`
* **Format:** 16:9 Widescreen (1920 × 1080), 30 fps, H.264 + AAC Stereo
* **Duration:** 30 seconds (succinct demo cut — optimal for LinkedIn muted-autoplay engagement)
* **Structure:** One photo (Tom's Palette dessert) walks through the real user flow:
  1. **Hook (0–4s):** "What if your dessert could teach you Spanish?"
  2. **Capture & Detect (4–10s):** AI tags `el gofre`, `el helado`, `el jarabe`, `el café` on the photo
  3. **Word Card (10–15s):** article, gender, example sentence for `el gofre`
  4. **Two-Way I-Spy (15–21s):** guess Linguini's clue, then describe one back
  5. **Journal (21–26s):** the lesson saves as a photo journal entry (+12 words, streak)
  6. **CTA (26–30s):** Public Beta — unlimited lessons, no card required
* **Design:** Numbered step badges, live "PUBLIC BETA" pill, dynamic bottom subtitle bar (for muted feed autoplay), and custom upbeat soundtrack.
* **Preview locally:** `npx hyperframes preview --background` inside `marketing/videos/linkedin-trailer/` → `http://localhost:3002/#project/linkedin-trailer`
* **Export when ready:** `npx hyperframes render` from the same folder (or press Export in Studio).

## 💡 Quick Tips for Maximum Reach on LinkedIn

1. **Upload the video file directly** as a native LinkedIn video (not a YouTube/Vimeo link). Native videos receive 4x–8x higher organic distribution, and the dynamic subtitle bar means it works fully muted.
2. **Select the thumbnail:** When uploading, set a custom thumbnail so the dessert photo hook is visible before playback starts.
3. **Tagging:** Tag your team members and professors using `@Ben Leong`, `@Yuen Jien Soo`, and `@Damith C. Rajapakse` so LinkedIn notifies them immediately.
4. **First 60 minutes:** Reply to every comment to push the post into 2nd-degree networks.
