## 2. Play I-Spy with your world

Learning a word is one thing. Spotting it, hearing a clue and describing it back is where it starts to stick. 👀

In Linguini, I-Spy goes both ways: first Linguini gives the clue, then it is your turn to describe something from the scene.

Would you rather give the clues or guess the answer?

Try it in Spanish or French through the link in our bio.
