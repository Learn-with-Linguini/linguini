# Image-generation prompts

All three images were generated with OpenAI's built-in image-generation tool, using repository assets as visual references. The resulting files were centre-cropped and resized to 1080 × 1350 for Instagram's 4:5 feed format.

## 1. Your desk is already a lesson

```text
Create a polished Instagram feed poster for the language-learning app Linguini.

OUTPUT: exactly a vertical 4:5 Instagram post, 1080 × 1350 pixels.

PURPOSE: evergreen awareness post, distinct from the supplied existing launch graphic.

USE THE REFERENCES:
- Preserve the supplied Linguini wordmark faithfully as the brand mark near the top.
- Use the supplied desk photo as the main photographic scene, but crop it confidently and warm it slightly.
- The supplied mascot/logo may appear once as a small supportive sticker, not as the main subject.

LAYOUT:
- Warm editorial composition on butter paper (#FFF9ED), with one large rounded photo window and restrained cream paper layers.
- Headline at upper left, large and extremely legible: “Your desk is already a lesson.”
- On the desk photo, add exactly four clean white vocabulary chips with tomato dots and dark ink text, each placed near the corresponding object:
  “el portátil”
  “el cuaderno”
  “el lápiz”
  “las gafas”
- Small bottom label: “Spanish from everyday photos”
- Add one looping pasta-yellow noodle gesture with tomato outline as a tasteful visual path, never crossing text.
- Palette: tomato #E85D32, pasta #F9B233, butter #FFF9ED, cream #FFF1D2, teal-dark #21716F, ink #263238.
- Rounded, human typography inspired by Baloo 2 for the headline and Nunito Sans for labels.
- Sunny, tactile, smart, playful but not childish. Premium student-built product campaign.
- Crisp margins and type; no mock phone, no gradients, no neon, no green-led palette, no owl or feathers.
- Do not add any words other than the exact text specified above and the supplied wordmark.
```

References: `frontend/public/brand/linguini-wordmark.png`, `frontend/public/linguini-logo.png`, `frontend/public/photos/desk-flatlay.jpg`.
