## Reply bank

General rules: reply to every comment. Thank people for specific things. Say "you're right" when they're right. Link to things instead of pasting long text. Never argue with a critic. Never ask for an upvote, not even jokingly. Everyone replies when free, but the launch lead or night voice signs off on anything about privacy, pricing or accuracy.

Where a question has two answers, the **preview** answer is for the curated-demo launch and the **full release** or **alternate** answer is for when the app flow is verified.

**“Can I use my own photo?”**
- Preview: “The public site currently offers a complete mini session using selected real photographs. We’re testing the user-photo flow in the learner app; we’ll update this answer when the production flow is verified.”
- Full release: “Yes. You can upload a photo or use your device camera in the browser. If an object suggestion is wrong, you can remove it before practising.”

**“How is this different from Duolingo?”**
> Good question, and Duolingo is great for a daily habit! The difference is where the words come from. In Duolingo everyone gets the same course. In Linguini the lesson comes from *your* photo: the café you're sitting in, your desk, the view from the bus. You choose which words to keep, play a 5-minute session on them, and your day goes into a journal. We think it pairs well with a course: learn the grammar there, and learn the words for your own life here. If you use both, we'd love to hear how they compare.

**“Which languages?” / “Which languages are next?”**
- Preview: “The interactive mini session offers Spanish and French from English. We’ll announce additional languages only when they are available.”
- Alternate: “Right now it's Spanish and French, taught from English. We're a small team and want those two to be really good before we add more. Which language would you want? Replies here count as votes for our roadmap.”
- *(The landing FAQ says "Italian is next on the list". Only repeat that if the team has committed to it.)*

**“Is there a mobile app?”**
> It runs in a browser on phone, tablet or laptop; there is no app-store download today.

**“What happens to my photo?” / “What about the privacy of my photos?”**
- Preview: “For the landing demo, you choose one of the site’s existing scenes. For personal uploads in the learner app, we’ll point you to the current privacy terms and confirm the exact storage and deletion behaviour before making a privacy guarantee here.”
- Alternate *(before launch, confirm the AI provider's data terms and link the privacy policy; see must-fix 2 in [plan.md](plan.md#must-fix-before-launch-day-ranked))*: “Your photos stay in your account. They're used only to build your sessions and your journal, and they're never shown to other learners. To find the objects, the photo is sent to our AI vision provider for analysis. Our privacy policy explains exactly what's sent and kept: [privacy policy link]. You can delete a journal entry at any time. If you'd rather not use your own photos, the ready-made scenes work just as well.”

**“Is the AI accurate?” / “Is the photo analysis AI?”**
- Preview: “The mini session uses curated sample scenes and content. We’re refining the photo analysis flow in the learner app, so we don’t want to describe the demo as live image recognition.”
- Alternate: “Mostly, but not always. That's why you review the words before every session: you untick anything wrong and tap the photo to add something it missed. The AI suggests, and you decide. Every word card shows the article, gender, meaning, IPA and audio, so you can check the details as well. If you catch a wrong word or translation, please tell us here with the word, and we'll fix it.”

**“What does it cost?”**
- Preview: “The interactive site demo is free to try. We’ll state learner-app pricing on the Product Hunt post only after the live billing and free limits are confirmed.”
- Alternate: “Linguini is free today: one new photo session a day, the journal, all the games and streaks, with no card needed. We plan a paid Plus tier later (unlimited sessions, pronunciation feedback, review mode). It isn't live yet, and nothing on Product Hunt today is behind a paywall.”
- *(Only use the "one session a day" line if the limit is actually enforced in the app. Otherwise say "free while we're in early launch". Pricing is an open decision in [listing.md](listing.md#pricing).)*

**“Why a journal?”**
> A new word is more memorable when it belongs to a day you remember. The journal is the place to reuse the words in your own sentence and return to them.

**Bug reports**
