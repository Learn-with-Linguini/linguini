# Linguini launch media

This directory contains four ordered Product Hunt gallery images, a social square, an icon derived from the repo's existing logo, and the original 18-second silent teaser. The **primary video for review and a future Product Hunt submission** is now the [30-second narrated launch film](../videos/launch-film/review.html); its source and export instructions are in the [video guide](../videos/launch-film/README.md). The silent teaser and a 50-second [animated explainer](explainer-script.md) remain available as alternates. All three videos are illustrative marketing pieces about the curated landing demo, rather than captured walkthroughs of personal-photo analysis. The graphics use the landing site's brand assets, sample photographs, and Spanish lesson/journal content.

The explainer is generated by `explainer.py` (visuals and timeline) and `soundtrack.py` (voice, music and effects). Setup and run commands are at the top of `explainer.py`. The voice is the Kokoro-82M `af_heart` voice (Apache-2.0), run locally. The music and effects are synthesised in code, so nothing needs licensing. The Python environment and model files live in the git-ignored `.venv/` and `.cache/`.

To review the primary film, serve the worktree root with `python3 -m http.server 8018 --bind 127.0.0.1` and open `http://localhost:8018/marketing/videos/launch-film/review.html`. The page links to the MP4, poster, captions and gallery. The production render must exist at `marketing/videos/launch-film/renders/linguini-launch-v2.mp4`; follow the [video build instructions](../videos/launch-film/README.md) if it has not been rendered yet. Product Hunt accepts a **YouTube URL** for video, so upload the approved MP4 there before adding it to a submission.

The banners are drawn by `banners.py` (run `python3 marketing/media/banners.py`), which reuses the helpers in `render.py` and pins each photo's words at the positions in `landing/data/scenes.ts`. It also writes web JPEGs to `landing/public/blog/launch/` for the launch blog post. The launch date is the `LAUNCH` constant at the top.

The launch post's animated blog-card cover is drawn by `launch_cover.py` (run `python3 marketing/media/launch_cover.py`, Pillow and ffmpeg). It animates the `blog-cover` composition: the real launch banners and gallery shots land one by one as a stack beside "Launch week.", then reset, in a seamless 9-second silent loop. It writes `landing/public/blog/launch/cover-loop.mp4` (1280 × 800, 30 fps) and `cover-loop-poster.jpg`, so run `banners.py` first if the banners change.

Regenerate the gallery and older silent teaser with `bash marketing/media/build_video.sh` (requires Pillow and ffmpeg). The teaser's intermediate stills are written to `export/teaser-frames/` and are not kept in the repo. Edit copy, colors, or composition in `render.py`. The gallery sequence is: promise → photo vocabulary → practice → journal and return habit.

The two photographs in the exports are attributed to **Viktor Hanáček / picjumbo** in [`landing/public/photos/credits.json`](../../landing/public/photos/credits.json). [Picjumbo's official FAQ](https://picjumbo.com/faq-and-terms/) says free photos may be used commercially (credit appreciated), and its [Golden Gate photo page](https://picjumbo.com/golden-gate-bridge-2/) names Hanáček. Before public upload, verify that the repository files match those source images and review any depicted property or brand rights. Replace a photograph with a team-owned image if rights cannot be confirmed. Confirm the live demo and URL still match the artwork and final video frame.
