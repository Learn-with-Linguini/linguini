# Linguini launch film audio

Original 30-second, 120 BPM instrumental score and sound design for the fast-paced launch ad. The build uses Python's standard library only and synthesizes every tone, drum and texture. **No third-party recordings, samples, stock music or external licences are involved.** Music and SFX are separate stereo 44.1 kHz PCM stems so the editor can duck music under the narration and rebalance effects. `preview_mix.wav` is a no-voice review mix, not the final vocal mix.

Run from the repository root:

```sh
python3 marketing/videos/launch-film/audio/build_audio.py
```

| File | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| `music.wav` | Warm instrumental groove: electric-piano style chords, rounded bass, organic drums, small mallet motif and a resolving outro. |
| `sfx.wav` | Camera shutter, scene moves, word pops, correct-answer ding, page turn and four-note sonic logo. |
| `preview_mix.wav` | Music + SFX for timing review; contains no narration. Generated by the build and not committed. |
| `cues.json` | Cut times, SFX cues, tempo and measured peak/RMS levels. |

All cuts at **0 / 2 / 5 / 8 / 12 / 16 / 19 / 23 / 27 / 30 seconds** land on the 0.5-second beat grid. Opening camera shutter starts near 0; word labels pop at 5, 5.5, 6, 6.5 and 7; the I-Spy correct click and paired ding land at **14.85** to follow the answer animation; the music still accents the 16-second cut. Seven soft sentence-token taps run from 19.2 through 21.0 in 0.3-second steps. A page turn lands at 23; the sonic logo begins at 27 and resolves into the final three seconds. Four brief lift points lead into selected cuts.

The instrumental measures approximately **-26.3 LUFS integrated, -9.2 dBFS true peak**. The no-voice preview measures approximately **-25.0 LUFS integrated, -7.0 dBFS true peak**. These levels intentionally leave room for the spoken narration. Set final program loudness after the narration is mixed, and trim the SFX stem around words if needed.
