# Linguini launch film — 30-second script

**Creative direction:** A real street photograph acquires new names, then turns into a tiny story. The cheerful Linguini bowl mascot narrates from a small circular avatar in the lower-right corner (about 12% of frame width), with simple mouth shapes synced to the voice. Keep the photo and app interaction dominant. English voiceover, with the Spanish and French words pronounced clearly. Fast, warm, curious; no exaggerated “AI magic” claim.

**Source and truth:** Record the *curated* `hillside-street` mini session on the live landing site. The demo includes `el coche` and `la voiture`, word review/audio, I-Spy, a gap sentence, a sentence builder, and a sample journal page. Do not show a new user photo being analysed or imply that the demo journal is saved to an account. End at `https://linguini-landing.vercel.app/`.

**Runtime:** Exactly **30.0 seconds**, 120 BPM, 60 musical beats at 0.5 seconds each. All nine visual boundaries are on a beat. Start the hook at frame one. Make each cut land with a short musical hit or sound accent; keep the upbeat instrumental under the narration. Leave no dead air between lines. Burn in full accessible voiceover captions separately from the short headline overlays listed below.

## Exact voiceover (70 words)

> That red car has another name. El coche in Spanish. La voiture in French. The street hasn't changed. The words have. Pick the words you want. Hear them in context. Now Linguini plays I-Spy. Can you find that car? Next, turn those words into a sentence. Build it word by word. Get it right. Then watch this street become a journal page. Try Linguini. Learn the language of your day.

No invented performance statistic, testimonial, paywall, voice recognition, or user-photo analysis in this cut.
