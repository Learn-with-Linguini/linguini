# Linguini human-voice campaign scripts

Three original, shootable concepts for the **curated photo mini session** at https://linguini-landing.vercel.app/ . Record a real narrator. The copy is meant to sound like a person noticing and deciding, with a warm first half and a firm invitation at the end. Each table gives the exact spoken line, its delivery window, and the image that earns it. Hold the final word inside its window; leave the remaining fraction of a second to music and the cut.

The 30-second film is the hero option for Product Hunt and the landing page. The 20-second cut is a stronger social argument; the 15-second cut is a product-first opener. The six-second hooks are separate paid/organic openers, not cropped endings of the longer scripts. All have a visible **Try the mini session** CTA and landing URL at the end.

## A · The world is already speaking · 30 seconds

**Idea:** One person moves through the ordinary world with new attention. Sound begins almost intimate: traffic, footsteps, a breath. The music grows only when the photo turns into a usable word. This is an invitation to notice, not a feature tour.

**Exact voiceover:** “The world is already speaking. That red car. Those flowers. A street you almost missed. El coche in Spanish. La voiture in French. Linguini lets you step inside a scene and learn its words. Find one. Build a sentence. And watch the moment become a journal page. The ordinary feels new again. Start where you are. Try Linguini’s mini session.”

| Seconds | Spoken sentence / phrase | Picture | Sound and on-screen copy |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0.0–2.5 | “The world is already speaking.” | Close on a real person stopping mid-walk and looking up. Faces and ambient light, no phone yet. | Bare city sound. **THE WORLD IS ALREADY SPEAKING.** |
| 2.5–4.0 | “That red car.” | Hard cut to the red car in the licensed hillside-street demo photo. | Single low drum hit. |
| 4.0–5.5 | “Those flowers.” | Close crop of flowers in the same photograph. | A second, lighter hit. |
| 5.5–7.5 | “A street you almost missed.” | Reveal the whole curated photo on a phone as the person taps its tile in the Linguini landing demo. | Street ambience folds into a restrained pulse. |
| 7.5–10.0 | “El coche in Spanish.” | Actual demo word marker/card for the car; make the article readable. | Cut the music down for pronunciation. **EL COCHE** |
| 10.0–12.5 | “La voiture in French.” | Switch the demo language; same car, different word. | Music returns under the phrase. **LA VOITURE** |
| 12.5–17.0 | “Linguini lets you step inside a scene and learn its words.” | Human reaction intercut with a real capture of the demo review screen and one word-card audio tap. | Percussion builds. Keep UI legible for two seconds. |
| 17.0–20.0 | “Find one. Build a sentence.” | I-Spy answer, then actual sentence-builder tokens from the hillside scene. | Two clean rhythmic impacts; no busy interface montage. |
| 20.0–24.0 | “And watch the moment become a journal page.” | Demo journal page appears with the hillside photo and its Spanish text. Visible small label: **SAMPLE JOURNAL**. | Music opens into a fuller chord; page-turn sound. |
| 24.0–26.5 | “The ordinary feels new again.” | Return to the person, now looking around with quiet recognition; hold their expression. | One breath of space. |
| 26.5–30.0 | “Start where you are. Try Linguini’s mini session.” | Linguini wordmark, warm brand background, site URL and one clear button. | Human voice becomes decisive; final music rise and stop. **TRY THE MINI SESSION** |

**Performance:** First line close and low, as if speaking to one friend. Give the Spanish and French words their full shape. Let “The ordinary feels new again” land without a smile in the voice; turn outward and confident on the final invitation. No shouted trailer voice.

## B · Stop collecting words. Start using them · 20 seconds

**Idea:** A sharp contrast between a word without a place and a word inside a scene. Start with the familiar frustration of a forgotten list, then snap into colour and action. The final line is a challenge.

**Exact voiceover:** “Another list. Another word gone by tomorrow. Give it a place. A red car becomes el coche. Find it in I-Spy. Put it in a sentence. Now it belongs to a story. Stop collecting words. Start using them. Try Linguini.”

| Seconds | Spoken sentence / phrase | Picture | Sound and on-screen copy |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0.0–1.5 | “Another list.” | Human hand flips a plain paper vocabulary card. Neutral light; no rival app or brand. | Dry card flick. **ANOTHER LIST.** |
| 1.5–3.5 | “Another word gone by tomorrow.” | Same person cannot recall the card, sets it down. Keep the performance small and believable. | Room sound drops for half a beat. |
| 3.5–5.5 | “Give it a place.” | Hard match cut to the hillside-street demo photo filling the frame. | Colour and bass arrive together. **GIVE IT A PLACE.** |
| 5.5–8.0 | “A red car becomes el coche.” | Car marker/card lands over the actual curated scene. | Make **el coche** readable; voice leads the edit. |
| 8.0–11.0 | “Find it in I-Spy.” | Show the real clue and the correct `el coche` choice. | One tactile answer hit. |
| 11.0–14.0 | “Put it in a sentence.” | Actual token sequence ends with `El coche rojo está en la calle.` | Percussion follows the token placements; hold the completed sentence. |
| 14.0–17.0 | “Now it belongs to a story.” | Sample journal page with the street photo; cut to the person’s small satisfied reaction. | Music lifts, then leaves space. **A WORD WITH A PLACE.** |
| 17.0–20.0 | “Stop collecting words. Start using them. Try Linguini.” | Bold wordmark, URL and **Try the mini session** CTA. | Three clear accents on **Stop / Start / Try**; decisive ending. |

**Performance:** Start tired, never sarcastic. Say “Give it a place” like a discovery. The final three imperatives build in conviction but stay conversational. Do not copy an existing ad’s cadence or chant.

## C · One photo. First words · 15 seconds

**Idea:** A small, satisfying first success. Minimal cast, product in hand, immediate payoff. This is the clearest short cut for viewers who have never heard of Linguini.

**Exact voiceover:** “One photo. One word you can see. El coche. Find the car. Build the sentence. Watch the street become a journal page. Your first words start here. Try Linguini.”

| Seconds | Spoken sentence / phrase | Picture | Sound and on-screen copy |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0.0–2.0 | “One photo.” | Full-frame curated hillside-street photo; a human thumb chooses it from the landing demo. | Camera-like click, then a warm note. **ONE PHOTO.** |
| 2.0–4.0 | “One word you can see.” | Car becomes the focal point without pretending to scan a newly taken image. | Light visual focus, no fake “AI processing” overlay. |
| 4.0–6.0 | “El coche.” | Actual Spanish word card large and legible; a real narrator gives it space. | Duck music under pronunciation. **EL COCHE** |
| 6.0–9.0 | “Find the car. Build the sentence.” | I-Spy answer, then sentence tokens; one interaction per beat. | Two precise hits. |
| 9.0–12.0 | “Watch the street become a journal page.” | Sample journal page from the demo; keep **SAMPLE JOURNAL** visible. | Page turn and warmer chord. |
| 12.0–15.0 | “Your first words start here. Try Linguini.” | Face looking up from phone, cut to brand end card and URL. | Voice lands on **here**, then a confident **Try Linguini**. **TRY THE MINI SESSION** |

**Performance:** Curious and immediate, like showing someone a neat thing you just found. Make “El coche” the audible turning point. The CTA is direct, never breathless.

## Product and reference notes for the editor

- The landing mini session uses **curated, ready-made scenes**. The red car, `el coche` / `la voiture`, I-Spy clue, sentence and journal draft all come from `landing/data/scenes.ts` and `landing/components/try/Session.tsx`. Present the journal as a sample demo page. Show the demo’s real controls or clearly stylised recreations of its *curated* content. These scripts make no claim of live analysis of a new photo or speech grading.
- The reference supplied by the team is [Zoom’s “I Use Zoom!” video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WNIDnCv9t7I). The video was not accessible for direct inspection here. [Zoom’s own campaign note](https://news.zoom.com/zoom-ahead-brand-campaign/) describes a dramatic, human-led spot built around an everyday frustration; that is the level of inspiration used here. The Linguini premises, words, scenes and lines are original. Use no Zoom footage, music, dialogue, costumes or actor imitation.
