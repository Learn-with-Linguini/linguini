## Overview

Warm tactile camera-roll collage with tomato-and-pasta accents. The mascot behaves like a reaction sticker rather than a presenter. Type is oversized, conversational and readable on a phone.

## Composition Rules

- One joke or claim per beat.
- Keep primary copy inside the safe rectangle and use the centre-left for headlines.
- Use photos as large paper cards, with short vocabulary chips appearing one at a time.
- Use smooth long-tail motion; a single playful mascot tilt is the only overshoot-like gesture.
- Hold the final CTA long enough to read.

## Do Not

- No generic blue SaaS gradients, fake phone chrome, dense feature lists or tiny captions.
- No important text in the right-side control rail or bottom native-caption zone.
- No endless loops, random particles or lazy breathing.
