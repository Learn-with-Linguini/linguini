## Intent

Create two cute, quick and self-aware vertical videos for Linguini's first public-beta week. The humour should feel native to TikTok and Reels: POV framing, a small relatable study joke, fast readable beats and the noodle mascot as the reaction character. Both cuts must make sense without sound.

## Assets

- `assets/camera-roll-key-art.png` — campaign key art for the camera-roll hook.
- `assets/desk-flatlay.jpg` — the real desk scene used for the photo-to-lesson demo.
- `assets/linguini-logo.png` — mascot sticker and final brand beat.
- `assets/linguini-wordmark.png` — brand lockup on both CTAs.

## Customizations

- Build two self-contained 15-second cuts in one 30-second review composition.
- Keep important text out of the right-side control rail and bottom caption area.
- Make the edit compatible with adding a current native TikTok or Reel sound at upload time.

## Notes

- No voice-over and no copyrighted trend audio.
- Avoid corporate-demo pacing, fake testimonials, competitor mimicry, owls and green-led branding.
- Current claims only: photo-led lessons; learner-reviewed vocabulary; Spanish, French and German plus beginner Korean; unlimited photo lessons during public beta.
